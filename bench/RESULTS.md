## What changed because of this

- Before the single-stage cap in `scorer.py`, the false-positive rate was **10.9 %** on the week dataset and **9.3 %** on the month: every nightly backup job (one High `T1041` alert, nothing else) was scored High and would have paged someone. Capping one-signature, one-tactic incidents at 59 brought it to 0 % with recall unchanged.
- `--pivot-src-ip` merges the lateral-movement hops in the attack chains (133 to 124 incidents on the week), which is the difference between seeing one intrusion and seeing two unrelated alerts on two hosts.

## What this does not prove

The data is synthetic. The generator is 150 lines and the attack chains are always multi-stage, so recall of 100 % says the scorer does what it was designed to do, not that it would catch a slow, single-stage intrusion. Treat these numbers as a regression gate (they run in CI) and as a floor, not as a claim about production detection. The next step is replaying Atomic Red Team runs through Suricata and Elastic in the lab and feeding the `eve.json` through `triage --format suricata`.
